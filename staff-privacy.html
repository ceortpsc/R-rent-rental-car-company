Staff privacy and access notice

Source review: October 30, 2026. Pending operational review before activation.

Implementation and release status

This notice describes the staff management code in review, not a certification that a live service is enabled. Management remains locked until the dedicated database, identity provider, email delivery, bootstrap and independent release review are verified. Public rental accounts and financial, signing, insurance and vehicle-release actions retain separate gates.

Identity and authenticator information

When management is enabled, the server sends sign-in credentials, email verification codes and authenticator codes to Supabase Auth for verification. The provider manages identity and MFA factors. R-Rent uses the verified user ID, confirmed email, provider session ID and MFA assurance to bind access to an active staff membership in the configured tenant. An email address alone does not grant a role.

Authenticator enrollment displays a setup secret only during setup; it is cleared when hidden or when the page becomes hidden. Do not copy secrets into access reasons, email, screenshots or support requests. MFA reset, email changes and owner recovery require independently verified security review.

Session cookies and access lifetime

Management uses host-only Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict cookies named __Host-rr_access, __Host-rr_refresh and __Host-rr_session. The browser sends these cookies to the same-origin server; application JavaScript does not read them or store tokens in localStorage or sessionStorage. The database stores a SHA-256 digest of the opaque management session secret, linked user/provider session IDs, timestamps and revocation state.

Management sessions expire after eight hours or twenty minutes of inactivity. Sign-out revokes the current management session; sign-out-all and password changes revoke all local management sessions. Provider logout is also attempted. These actions do not certify sign-out from other applications or devices outside this management service. Cookie expiry and access revocation are not deletion of retained database records.

Membership, invitations and audit records

The management database records tenant membership, role, active status and version; requested access changes and reasons; proposer and independent approver IDs; invitation email, role, expiry, delivery state and acceptance identity; and release evidence references. The audit ledger records actor ID, action, bounded reason or record reference and timestamp. Authorized staff can view tenant-scoped lists according to role; the interface retrieves up to 100 recent records.

Audit calls omit passwords, provider tokens, verification codes, TOTP secrets and raw request bodies. Access-change reasons are retained separately, so enter only the minimum business reason and never paste personal documents. The rate limiter stores hashed action/IP and action/identity buckets with attempt counts and window timestamps. These hashes are security data, not a promise of anonymization. Hosting and identity providers may maintain their own security logs.

Providers and access restrictions

The implementation uses same-origin hosting, Supabase Auth and database services, and configured provider email delivery for invitation and sign-in messages. Dedicated project binding, SMTP, hosted MFA, provider logging, regions, subprocessors and backup behavior remain unverified. Only server service credentials can use the private management RPCs; protected actions require verified identity, active tenant membership, AAL2 and role permission. Privilege changes require a distinct authorized approver.

Retention and privacy requests

No automatic management-record deletion schedule is implemented in this source migration. Invitation expiry and session expiry prevent use but do not delete records. Before activation, the responsible owner must document approved retention periods, deletion and backup procedures, legal holds, provider terms and the staff privacy-request workflow. No fixed deletion deadline or completed live privacy review is claimed here.

For a staff privacy inquiry, contact ceo@rosstaxsoftware.com with the subject R-Rent Staff Privacy Request. This existing executive address is not a verified secure privacy portal. Describe the request without credentials, authenticator codes, government ID or complete financial details. Identity and authority must be verified through an approved process before any disclosure, correction or deletion.

Safe staff procedure

Read this notice before signing in. Use only an approved invitation or account and an expiring provider setup link/code; no shared or plaintext password distribution is authorized. Verify the intended site, sign in, complete your own authenticator verification, then confirm the server-assigned role. Stop if the role or tenant is unexpected.

Propose a minimal access change, obtain independent approval, and have an authorized operator separately review any invitation delivery. Never use another account belonging to the same person as the independent approver. Rental execution, binding signatures, financial transactions and vehicle release require their own verified evidence and authorization. Sign out on shared devices and report unexpected access through the established company security process.
