# R-Rent | Privacy Policy

**Document:** RR-LGL-002  
**Effective:** October 10, 2026  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Operator:** Ross Tax Pro Software Company, Rental Car Division  
**Status:** Published informational website policy; binding rental documents require legal and insurance review.

> The current R-Rent site processes basic website and quote traffic and only receives customer emails when they are actually sent.

## 1. Who we are and how to contact us

This Privacy Policy covers the public R-Rent website operated by Ross Tax Pro Software Company, Rental Car Division, at rtpscrentalcars.com. It describes actual current information flows separately from planned rentals, protected accounts and integrations.

For privacy inquiries use ceo@rosstaxsoftware.com with the subject R-Rent Privacy Request. This is an existing Company executive contact, not yet a verified dedicated secure privacy portal. Do not send a government identification scan, SSN, insurance policy image, complete card number or security code by ordinary email.

## 2. What currently works—and what does not

The website currently provides public fleet information, a preliminary rental quote calculator, customer-service forms that prepare emails on the user's device, and browser-local signing practice. Secure renter accounts, driver-license uploads, insurance-file verification, payment checkout, certified digital signing and confirmed reservations have not been enabled for the general public.

Entering a quote does not register an account or reserve a vehicle. Opening a complaint or dispute form does not create an internal support record unless the visitor separately sends a message or a protected case service is later properly activated.

## 3. Information received during site visits

Hosting, network-delivery and security services may receive IP address, browser and device information, request URL, referring website, access time, approximate network location, error details and similar technical logs needed to serve and defend the site. The amount retained by providers depends on configured hosting and security controls.

The current reviewed public scripts do not install an advertising analytics SDK, marketing pixel or a public customer-account session cookie. Security systems or browsers may still use technical cookies, caches or other identifiers; the Company must reassess and update this notice if such behavior changes.

## 4. Estimate, support and practice-signature data

When a visitor calculates an estimate, the site sends a selected vehicle identifier and pickup/return date-times to an R-Rent pricing endpoint and receives a nonbinding calculation. The quote form does not ask for names, government ID numbers or payment credentials.

Feedback, review, complaint and dispute forms prepare text locally and open an email-draft workflow; the recipient may process the sender's address, message, rental reference, optional amount or date if the visitor actually sends it. The browser-local signature and initials demonstration is not a provider-certified electronic contract or remote signature storage service.

## 5. Data categories proposed for future rentals

Only after applicable services are approved and activated, R-Rent may need names, contact details, verified driver's-license attributes, authorized-driver eligibility, rental dates, vehicle assignment, condition photos, insurance coverage documents, payment-provider tokens and transaction references, signed agreement/audit records, damage claims, and customer-support records.

Driver-license, financial and insurance data may be sensitive. Before enabling those features, the Company must disclose the actual categories, purpose, recipient service providers, required verification and retention/deletion schedule, rights and any legally necessary consent. Public forms must not be used as substitutes for a secure upload vault.

## 6. Purposes for processing

Present technical data may be used to deliver pages, protect the site, prevent abuse and troubleshoot problems. Quote input is used to calculate a reference price. Voluntarily sent communications may be used to respond to the particular inquiry, billing concern or service request.

If future rental operations commence, data may be processed for identity and driving-eligibility checks, lawful contract administration, insurer validation, fraud prevention, payment/reconciliation, vehicle release, return inspection, claims, tax records, legal compliance and dispute resolution. Use of data for unrelated purposes must be separately assessed and disclosed.

## 7. Service providers and sharing

Website infrastructure and hosting providers may process technical logs as part of operating the website. When a visitor elects to send an email, the sender's and recipient's email systems process that communication. External linked sites have independent privacy practices.

Potential future providers may include identity and insurance verification vendors, card processors such as Stripe or PayPal, approved e-sign platforms, support systems, secure databases, auditors and lawful authorities. Naming a prospective category is not a claim that R-Rent is already sending renter data to those vendors. No presently reviewed public application code enables selling renter records or interest-based advertising; actual provider practices must be reassessed before launch.

## 8. Cookies, tracking and marketing choices

The reviewed public R-Rent scripts do not currently advertise or implement behavioral advertising controls, targeted advertising pixels or a functional marketing preference center. Browser settings can restrict cookies and stored data, though essential web security or session tools may be affected.

Before introducing analytics, profiling or targeted advertising, R-Rent must document the vendors, categories of data, purposes and legally required opt-out/consent controls. No privacy-preference control will be labeled as working until a real backend or consent manager enforces it.

## 9. Sensitive documents, payment data and security

Driver-license files, insurance declarations, dates of birth and payment information should only be collected using approved authenticated services with least-privilege access, encryption, malware screening, audit logs and a retention policy. These systems are currently unavailable in the public preview.

R-Rent should not receive full payment card numbers or security codes in support forms or ordinary email. Future payment components must be provided by authorized processors with verified fraud and webhook controls. No internet service is completely risk-free; possible personal-data incidents will require investigation and notices where applicable.

## 10. AI analysis and significant decisions

R-Rent plans AI assistance for administrative case triage, drafting and completeness checks. Such tools are not currently represented as automatic eligibility adjudication, insurance approval, payment settlement or legal contract execution. Human-authorized decisions and verifiable evidence remain required.

If profiling or automated processing that significantly affects individuals is introduced, the Company must assess additional privacy disclosures, rights, contest/appeal options, model access boundaries and retention before activation. Sensitive renter documents should not be sent to general-purpose AI without an authorized secure integration.

## 11. Retention, browser behavior and deletion

Browser-local customer-care drafts and signature examples are not submitted to an R-Rent account database through the public page. Browsers may retain cache/history, and transmitted emails may persist with sender/recipient email services. Hosting/security access records may be retained according to provider configuration and applicable requirements.

A final retention schedule for future license images, rental agreements, insurance evidence, payments and disputes has not yet been verified and therefore is not represented as active. The Company must adopt category-specific retention and deletion rules covering security, taxes, contractual obligations and litigation holds before enabling sensitive rental processing.

## 12. Privacy rights and requests

Depending on applicable law and the Company's coverage, individuals may have rights to confirm processing, access, correct, delete, obtain copies of personal data, or opt out of defined targeted advertising, sale or certain profiling. The Texas Data Privacy and Security Act, Business & Commerce Code Chapter 541, establishes notice, request and appeal rules for covered controllers, subject to applicable exemptions and thresholds.

To request an applicable right, email ceo@rosstaxsoftware.com with R-Rent Privacy Request and describe the desired action and safe reply method. Do not attach an ID scan in the initial email. The Company may need proportionate verification through a secure method. If a request is refused, any statutory appeal and regulator-complaint processes that apply must be explained and followed; the website does not falsely promise a live self-service privacy dashboard.

## 13. Minors, geography and third-party links

This site is intended for general informational browsing, not for collecting children's sensitive identity information or entering vehicle rental contracts with unlicensed drivers. The Company's actual minimum renter age and insurance conditions must be approved and published before rental processing.

R-Rent currently focuses on Texas and U.S. vehicle rentals. Internet traffic may pass through hosting infrastructure in other regions. Linked email providers, vehicle manufacturers and third-party sites have their own privacy policies and may use their own processing locations.

## 14. Updates, effective date and contact

Effective October 10, 2026; version 1.0.0. Material new features—including live OAuth registration, secure file uploads, payment processing, complaint case filing, tracking pixels or insurance verification—require revised privacy disclosures and any applicable consent/rights controls before launch. Policy revisions should be versioned and dated.
