# R-Rent Draft Customer/Operating Policies — not issued

**Driver standards:** valid appropriate driving license, legally/insurer-approved minimum age and any driving-record conditions; additional drivers require separate eligibility. No relaxation based on customer requests.

**Insurance:** verify company commercial rental policy and customer coverage where relevant; a card/declarations upload does not prove coverage. Coverage must be in force for each agreed driving period. Disclose actual insurer, limits, exclusions and claims route, and separately elect optional insurance or non-insurance products.

**Pricing:** publish exact clock-based period, base rate, mandatory non-optional fees, location-specific taxes, optional charges, refundable hold, pickup/return rules and total before acceptance. No surprise mandatory fees. Any optional waiver must have required written election and mandated notices.

**Use / possession:** only authorized drivers; pickup condition photos, verified odometer/fuel, check engine and damage list, driver instructions and verified support contact. Standard traffic safety duties. Return inspection based on real evidence.

**Extensions / cancellation / refunds:** company must approve extensions, extend validated insurance period and return a revised final amount before use; publish lawful cancellation terms; refund optional waiver days when §91.057 applies; release deposit/card holds under disclosed provider timeline.

**Damage / incident / roadside:** protect safety, contact emergency services if necessary, follow insurer claim procedures, document actual charges, preserve dispute rights and prompt investigation. No blanket claim that a waiver eliminates every possible obligation.

**Electronic communications:** consent must be informed, documented and revocable where applicable. Provide final e-signed packet to company and customer; no unverified email documents.

**Data security:** driver documents, payment metadata and insurance documents are confidential restricted data; segregate tenants, limit retention, allow access logs and approved correction/deletion processes, comply with incident notices.

**Disputes:** offer an accessible complaint channel, itemized evidence of extra charges, staff escalation and timely payment processor dispute handling. Do not subject renters to automated-only irreversible decisions.

**Third-party hosts:** excluded until separate underwriting, coverage and Chapter 113 review.
