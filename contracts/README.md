# R-Rent contract and addendum pack — UNSIGNED TEMPLATES

These are internal draft templates requiring review by a qualified Texas lawyer and insurer. They are not signed, legally approved or binding customer documents. Each issued package must contain controlled revision/hash, actual vehicle and dates, an exact itemized final quote, driver and coverage evidence, all mandated notices, customer-specific acknowledgement, provider-backed signature and accessible customer/company copies.
